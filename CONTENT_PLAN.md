**Primary goal:** Rank for "AI smart display," "AI agent device," and "smart home AI" queries.
**Secondary goal:** Get cited in AI search engines (Google AI Overviews, ChatGPT, Perplexity) for smart display and AI agent queries.

## Publishing Schedule

**Weeks 1-2 (launch):** Publish the 5 seed posts (already done).
**Weeks 3-6:** 2 posts/week from Clusters 1 and 2 (informational, top-of-funnel).

**Ongoing:** 1-2 posts/week, rotating clusters. Update existing posts quarterly.

## SEO Formatting Rules (Every Post)

1. **H1:** Post title, includes primary keyword, under 60 chars ideal
2. **H2s:** Question format where possible, include secondary keywords
3. **First 50-70 words:** Direct answer to the main question (for AI Overview citation)
4. **One statistic per 150-200 words** with source attribution
5. **Comparison tables** in every comparison post (HTML `<table>` for snippet eligibility)
6. **Internal links:** 3-5 per post, linking to related cluster posts and the product page
7. **CTA at bottom:** Every post ends with a product CTA box
8. **Tags:** 4-6 per post, matching target keywords
9. **Reading time:** Displayed on every post
10. **Publication date:** Displayed prominently (AI engines favor fresh content)
11. **Article schema (JSON-LD):** Auto-generated per post via `generateMetadata`
